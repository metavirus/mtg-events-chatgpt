## Initial comparative conclusion

The communities serve different roles:

1. **InfiniteLoopMTG** currently offers the strongest observed live
   LFG/pre-arrangement mechanism, but much of its activity is farther north and less
   convenient from Los Alamitos.
2. **MTG OC** appears most aligned with the user's Bracket 2/3 and newcomer-friendly
   preferences, though it is smaller and less frequently active.
3. **Legendary Creature Club** has rich structure and high activity, and its Long
   Beach-centered geography gives it a meaningful convenience advantage over
   InfiniteLoopMTG. Its event information is noisier and still needs a targeted
   scheduled-event/LFG review before a confident overall fit assessment.

These are source/community assessments, not final venue or event rankings.
