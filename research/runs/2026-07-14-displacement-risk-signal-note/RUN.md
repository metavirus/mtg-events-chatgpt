## Objective

Capture the clarified interpretation of how we should detect likely routine-event
displacement without demanding impossible proof.

## Captured clarification

- The strongest practical displacement signal is often a prominently advertised
  same-day major event.
- Example: a loud `BIG RCQ TOURNAMENT ON FRIDAY!` style post.
- If such an event would plausibly consume store space, staffing, or player
  attention, it is enough to mark routine Commander as `at risk` for that date
  even if no explicit cancellation notice appears.

## Reconciliation decision

Use major same-day events as warning signals, not just explicit `Commander is
canceled` language.
