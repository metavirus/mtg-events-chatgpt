- The Wizards-normalized `Commander all day / No proxy` signal remains the
  defining user-fit caution and should be preserved.

    the gathering cards`

    County for your MTG needs`

    rooms used to expand capacity on large tournament days

  owners/staff and at least some gaming-table presence.
- Facebook is real but the visible page slice appears older and more
  tournament-oriented than community/Commander-social in the current bounded

  than weakening it prematurely.
- Upgrade the broader store interpretation: SoCalMagic appears to be a serious
  Magic-first store with meaningful MTG depth, not just a random nearby listing.
- This means the store should stay important in the full map even if it ends up
  ranking low for the user's typical proxy-friendly casual Commander goals.
