## Why this run exists

The project policy was refined on 2026-07-14 to allow user-supplied field notes
to live in the repo-backed evidence layer as clearly attributed sources. This
run captures the first such note so the information is not lost to chat-context
or delayed private-app implementation.

- Related recurring series:
  - `finch-sparrow-commanderfest-6-14-30`

## Attribution

Source type: `userFieldNote`
Attribution: direct user report in project conversation
Recorded date: 2026-07-14

## Captured observations

- The user attended Finch and Sparrow for two prereleases plus Saturday
  structured casual Commander and Sunday casual/open-play Commander.
- Saturday structured casual Commander was described as a reasonable experience.
- Saturday used random four-player pod assignment.
- Saturday used loose 50-minute rounds.
- Saturday was fun overall, but offered less control over who and what power
  level the user played against.
- Some very experienced players and occasionally very strong decks were in the
  Saturday mix.
- Sunday casual/open-play Commander had no structure.
- Sunday open play was described as generally having the right vibe and being a
  decent experience.

## Analytical significance

- Finch appears to support at least two materially different Commander social
  modes:
  - structured pod-assignment Commander
  - unstructured open-play Commander
- This is exactly the kind of information public listings often fail to convey.
- The note should influence future solo-arrival analysis, event-fit assessment,
  and "before you go" guidance without overwriting official event descriptions.

## Follow-up implications

- Add a normalized path for attributed user field notes in generated data.
- Later model Sunday open play more explicitly if reliable current source support
  or additional user field notes confirm it as an ongoing pattern.
- Preserve future user field notes in the same manner for other venues.
