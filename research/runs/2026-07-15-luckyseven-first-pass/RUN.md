# Research Run: Lucky Seven Games First Pass

Date: 2026-07-15

## Objective

Run the first real cross-source pass on Lucky Seven Games and determine whether
it should remain a bare Wizards-discovery lead or be promoted into a partial
record with a more useful Magic/community interpretation.

## Sources consulted

- Lucky Seven Games official website
- Lucky Seven Games Instagram profile
- Wizards Store & Event Locator listing and already-normalized event records
- Public discovery/community surfaces used only as light prominence context

## Findings

- This is clearly a real, operating TCG-first storefront rather than a thin or
  stale shell. The official site is current and commerce-heavy, with Magic: The
  Gathering foregrounded in navigation, singles, sealed inventory, and featured
  products.
- The official site meta description is unusually useful for a first pass: it
  explicitly says the location holds daily events and tournaments, and it names
  Magic alongside Pokemon, Lorcana, One Piece, and Yu-Gi-Oh.
- Wizards already gives two meaningful recurring Commander anchors:
  free-entry `Lucky 7 Casual Commander` on Sunday and Monday at 7:00 PM.
- Public discovery surfaced a real Instagram account
  (`@luckysevengames_ca`) with a modest footprint (roughly 292 followers and 12
  posts in the visible discovery slice). That is enough to count as a real
  social surface, but not enough yet to infer rich community pre-coordination or
  especially deep Commander chatter.
- The net first-pass pattern is: real store, real MTG commerce, real recurring
  Commander, but still a thin public community texture compared with some of the
  stronger social-calendar stores already promoted.

## Analytical read

- Promote Lucky Seven Games into a partial record now.
- Current best read: stronger than a generic Wizards-only lead because the
  official store surface clearly supports recurring organized play and a real
  Magic product ecosystem.
- At the same time, the current public social layer looks light, so
  meetup-accessibility and community-continuity should stay moderate rather than
  inflated until richer social/Discord/current-event evidence appears.

## Follow-up candidates

- Look for a cleaner current event-calendar or registration surface if one
  exists beyond the main storefront.
- If a Discord or stronger social/event routing source emerges later, perform
  the standard bounded Discord/social first-pass overlay.
