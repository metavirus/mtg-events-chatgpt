## Findings and insights

### Exact evidence

- The official Wednesday and Friday Fountain Valley event pages both use
  `Commander Slay Pass` terminology for weekly Commander nights.
- Those event pages describe:
  - paid entry
  - fixed check-in and start times
  - store-assisted pod filling
  - explicit house rules
- Separate Kingslayer-branded WPN text explains:
  - `What does a Slay Pass entry get me? Each Slay Pass player will receive 3 Magic the Gathering Booster Packs ... after joining the event through the MTG Companion app`

### Normalized facts

- `Slay Pass` should currently be interpreted as Kingslayer house terminology,
  not as a separate Magic format.
- For Fountain Valley Commander, the practical meaning is a paid, structured
  weekly Commander event entry with staff-managed seating help and house rules.
- Booster-pack support may be part of the broader Kingslayer Slay Pass model,
  but that specific reward wording has not yet been tied cleanly enough to the
  Fountain Valley branch to promote as branch-specific fact.

### Analytical interpretation

- This is a good example of the app needing a translation layer: unfamiliar
  store jargon should be converted into player-understandable meaning.
- `Slay Pass` sounds exotic, but the currently preserved evidence makes it look
  more like a branded admission package than a distinct play style.
- The remaining ambiguity is not what the event is; it is whether every
  Kingslayer branch attaches the same prize-support package to the term.

### Personal-fit assessment

- Kingslayer remains promising for structured solo arrival because staff
  assistance with pod completion is directly stated.
- The house rules also suggest a deliberately constrained casual environment,
  which may be friendlier than an unrestricted high-power room.
- The paid structured model may still feel different from free-form open play,
  so lived-experience refinement remains worthwhile.

### Confidence and ambiguity

- High confidence: `Slay Pass` is Kingslayer-specific terminology rather than a
  broader Magic format label.
- High confidence: Fountain Valley Wednesday and Friday Commander are structured
  paid events with pod-filling help and explicit house rules.
- Medium confidence: booster-pack support is part of the broader Kingslayer
  Slay Pass concept.
- Low/medium confidence: that exact booster-pack support wording is branch-clean
  enough to assign directly to Fountain Valley without another confirming source.
