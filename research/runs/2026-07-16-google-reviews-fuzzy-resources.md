## Method and evidence rules

This pass is separate from the Yelp pass. Direct Google Maps pages were used where indexed access exposed them. Otherwise, Google-derived ratings and review excerpts were captured from mirrors or aggregators that explicitly identify Google as the source, including Birdeye, Card Shop Hub, Card Shops Finder, CardShows.io, Chamber of Commerce, Wanderlog, specialty card-shop directories, and other business-profile mirrors.

Every record below preserves the exact source URL, displayed rating/review count, branch/address identity, and the claim supported. Counts can drift over time and some mirrors lag Google. Conflicting counts are retained rather than silently reconciled. A mirror is not equivalent to a live Google Maps inspection and should be rechecked before canonical use.

## Additional resources surfaced by fuzzy search

The fuzzy pass identified several resource classes that should be incorporated into future store research routing:

1. **Google-derived specialist directories** — Card Shop Hub, Card Shops Finder, CardShows.io, KeepUp, PokémonCard.info, Board Game Cafe Finder. Useful for approximate Google rating/count, product categories, review excerpts, and discovery. They are secondary mirrors and require identity/freshness checks.
2. **Birdeye** — useful when it explicitly separates Google/Facebook review counts and exposes recent Google excerpts. Avoid treating its combined headline total as pure Google unless the breakdown supports that.
3. **Wanderlog and Chamber of Commerce** — useful for full Google-review excerpts and practical operational themes. Chamber surfaced the important Odyssey solo-arrival/clique concern.
4. **TCGplayer seller profiles and feedback** — strong evidence of active commerce, product depth, seller reliability, and sometimes unexpected MTG inventory emphasis; not evidence of in-person play by itself.
5. **Whatnot** — useful for live-commerce/community scale, follower counts, sales volume, and store identity; not a substitute for organized-play evidence.
6. **Official organized-play lists** — Yu-Gi-Oh!, Flesh and Blood, Pokémon, Lorcana and similar platform locators can confirm current physical operation, address, contact details, and event-hosting capability even when MTG sources are thin.
7. **Official Linktree/social routers** — especially valuable for multi-branch businesses such as The Game Chest because they preserve branch-specific Discord and Facebook identities.
8. **Local reporting** — necessary for nontraditional venues such as BYO Games, where community function may be better documented in journalism than in store calendars.
