## Summary

All four stores already have Commander records in Supabase. This pass did not
find a reason to add duplicate event rows. The value is Places-page assessment
and evidence visibility:

- Shadow Realm is close and real, with a Monday paid/prize-supported Commander
  anchor, but the source texture is mixed and still not clearly solo-friendly.
- Buddies has the richest routing and community surface in the batch, including
  official site, Linktree, Discord, Twitch/Whatnot/social routing, and weekly
  tournament infrastructure. It also has meaningful review cautions around the
  live-break/store business model, pricing, and busy-store service friction.
- TK is a real Newport Beach TCG shop with a Saturday casual Commander record,
  other organized-play locator support, and friendly/kid-friendly review
  signals, but its public footprint skews more Pokemon/collectibles than
  Magic-community depth.
- Aki is a real San Gabriel TCG shop with a Saturday Commander record and strong
  Pokemon/One Piece/commerce signals, but the current public evidence still
  supports MTG depth less strongly than broader TCG retail activity.
