## Why this correction exists

The ChatGPT packet is an intake aid and source lead. It is not the research
universe, and it is not a substitute for a reasonable store pass.

Recent misses exposed a pattern:

- Buddies Collectibles had a direct Yelp page with material review texture, but
  the pass leaned too much on secondary mirrors.
- Tilted Gaming had packet-derived review signals that influenced reasoning but
  were not visible as Evidence until corrected.
- B.Y.O.GAMES had local-journalism evidence supporting the community-lounge
  assessment but that source was not initially visible as Evidence.
- Grails Gone Wild had readily discoverable official/social/alias evidence that
  was missed because the pass stayed too narrowly inside the packet/status
  conflict lane.

The corrected rule is now recorded in `research/SOURCE_SOP.md`: every ordinary
store pass needs a lightweight baseline sweep across official, social,
community, organized-play, review, marketplace, and locator routes, with a
compact source-coverage line. A main store pass must inspect accessible useful
content and synthesize it. "Not found" is acceptable. Silently unexamined, or
merely found-but-unread, is not.
