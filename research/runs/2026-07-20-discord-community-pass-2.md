- Date: 2026-07-20
- Mode: bounded manual Discord/community content pass
- Scope: 5 high-value routes from Communities "Inspect next"
- Routes inspected: Collectors Lounge - Cypress, Hobby Overflow, Krazy Nick's Games, Kingslayer Games, Paper Hero's Games

## Purpose

This pass tested whether a small set of high-value Discord routes could improve planning confidence without becoming a broad Discord sweep. It focused on:

- current/high-value event signals;
- source-health contradictions;
- community texture, especially turnout, LFG, and "is it worth driving over?" clues;
- whether each route should remain in a regular monitoring queue.

Quiet or low-yield findings are recorded as run-level observations only. A quiet pass does not mean a route is permanently low-value.

### Paper Hero's Games

- Channels inspected: Huntington Beach `#magic-the-gathering`, all-location `#general`
- Content accessible: yes
- Last useful date observed: 2026-07-11 for bot-posted event reminders; 2026-07-09 for Standard time adjustment; 2026-07-20 for all-location general join activity
- Current run result: useful event discovery and source capture
- Route value: high
- Suggested monitoring cadence: regular while Paper Hero remains thin in the app

Findings:

- The Huntington Beach Magic channel is active enough to support event/source checking.
- A 2026-07-09 store/bot message said the weekly Standard event time was adjusted from 6 PM to 7 PM and that the change is reflected on the website calendar and Wizards schedule.
- A 2026-07-10 bot message announced Friday Night Magic booster draft at 6 PM, $20 entry, pack-per-round-win prizing, and Magic Companion registration.
- A 2026-07-11 bot message announced weekly Pauper at 7 PM, $5 entry, with store-credit prizing for top players.
- The all-location general channel mainly showed join/welcome noise and did not add planning value in this pass.

Interpretation:

- Paper Hero currently appears underrepresented in the app: Supabase only shows a Thursday Commander row for Paper Hero's Games - Huntington Beach.
- The Discord route should be captured as Evidence/community route.
- Saturday Pauper is a strong event-write candidate because the Discord post explicitly says "weekly Pauper tournament" and gives time, entry, and prizing.
- Friday FNM booster draft is a plausible event-write candidate, but the visible post is framed as "tonight." It should be proposed with moderate confidence unless confirmed by website/WPN during the write review.
- Weekly Standard at 7 PM should remain event-TBD until the day/location/source context is verified against the website or Wizards row, because the sampled Discord context suggests Standard but is not clean enough by itself.

Remaining unknowns:

- Whether the Friday booster draft is a stable weekly Huntington Beach row or a one-night FNM reminder.

- Whether Paper Hero has meaningful Commander/LFG texture beyond the existing Thursday Commander row.
