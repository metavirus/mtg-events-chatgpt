- Public membership indicator: approximately 1,185 members at discovery time.
- Relevance: ProjectCCG Santa Ana is in the store catalog and within the routine research area. The public profile also refers to multiple Southern California stores, so geographic scope needs confirmation inside the server.
- Source: https://linktr.ee/ProjectCCG
- Initial structure observed: separate Orange County store-information, announcements, events, and questions channels plus MTG announcements, discussion, and marketplace channels.
- Access note: the user selected the Orange County and Magic: The Gathering roles. The relevant channels were successfully opened on 2026-07-14. Discord's accessibility label still calls these `Private Channel (locked)`, but this denotes role-limited visibility rather than an actual remaining access failure.
- Initial event findings:
  - `oc-events` is actively maintained for the broader tournament schedule; the most recent visible schedule update was posted 2026-07-10.
  - `mtg-announcements` contains store-specific changes and cancellations that may not be recoverable from static calendars. Examples include a same-week Commander/draft cancellation, time changes for recurring Standard, and an event postponed in response to community availability.
  - Visible recurring signal: Standard locals on Tuesdays and Fridays at 6:30 PM as of the 2026-05-26 update.
  - Visible Commander signal: Commander appears to be part of the routine schedule, though the exact normal day/time still needs confirmation from the current schedule image or additional channel evidence.
  - Social-accessibility signal: a June MTG Pride Night explicitly offered either self-selected pods or staff placement into a pod, with casual or competitive play welcomed. This is a strong positive solo-arrival indicator, though it describes a special event rather than necessarily the routine Commander structure.
  - Special-event signal: the channel carried detailed prerelease notices with check-in time, start time, fee, kit limits, rounds, and prizing.
- Freshness note: general OC schedule communication was current through 2026-07-10; the latest visible post in `mtg-announcements` was 2026-06-29. This does not by itself show the MTG program is stale because the current schedule may be image-based in `oc-events`.
- Next action: inspect the current OC schedule image, MTG discussion freshness, store-information channel, and available member/online indicators; reconcile the current schedule against Wizards and other public sources.

### Magic The Gathering OC (Casual) — regional community

- Status: joined and confirmed in the `Stores/Local` folder as `MTG OC`.
- Public invite: https://discord.gg/a2mXqs3ptY
- Public membership indicator: Meetup listed 16 members at discovery time; Discord membership/online counts not yet observed.
- Relevance: very strong qualitative fit. The group explicitly welcomes new and casual Orange County players, includes Commander, provides introductory structure, and describes name tags, introductions, teaching, socializing, and store visits.
- Event evidence: the Meetup page listed recurring/monthly activity, including a Collector's Lounge Cypress Commander night and prerelease outing.
- Caveat: small group; activity and durability must be verified in Discord. Do not treat the public Meetup member count as the Discord member count.
- Source: https://www.meetup.com/magic-the-gathering-oc-casual/
- Initial structure observed: scheduled Events surface; announcements; monthly-meetings; events_activities; resources/tutorials; feedback/promotions; and a tabletop-simulator voice channel.
- Initial qualitative signal: strong social-accessibility fit. Visible welcome-channel activity included personal welcomes, encouragement to attend for an hour or two to meet people, and direction toward upcoming monthly meetings. The group appears intentionally small and organized rather than a high-volume general chat server.
- Privacy note: personally identifying contact details visible in the welcome channel were intentionally not retained in this research log.
- Next action: inspect monthly-meetings, events_activities, announcements, and the scheduled Events surface for freshness, attendance structure, locations, formats, and Commander relevance.
